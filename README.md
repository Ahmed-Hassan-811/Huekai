# Huekai 📸

**Step into anime worlds without being reincarnated.**

Huekai is a camera app that colour-grades your real photos so they *feel* like
they belong in a chosen anime world — Cyberpunk, One Piece, AOT, Ghibli, Solo
Leveling, Death Note, and more. It doesn't turn photos into cartoons; it re-grades
the light, colour and mood of the real shot, while keeping people's skin natural.

## ⬇️ Download

**[Download the latest test build (APK)](https://github.com/Ahmed-Hassan-811/Huekai/releases/latest)**

> Test build — your phone will warn about installing from an "unknown source."
> That's normal for a direct APK. Tap **Install anyway** to continue.

## What's inside

- **6 built-in looks**, each tuned to a different anime world
- **Studio** — make and edit your own looks with live sliders, save them, reuse them
- **Import .cube LUTs** from Lightroom / DaVinci Resolve, and **export** your own looks as .cube
- **Vault** — an in-app gallery of your shots, filterable by look
- Flash, front/back flip, zoom, share — a full camera
